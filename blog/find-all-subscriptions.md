# How to Find All Subscriptions Charged to Your Credit Card (Free Method)

**TL;DR:** The average person has 8-12 active subscriptions and remembers about 5. The fastest way to find the ones you forgot is to scan your Gmail for billing emails — every subscription sends receipts, and Gmail keeps them forever.

## Why you can't just check your bank statement

This is what most people try first. It doesn't work, for three reasons:

1. **Cryptic merchant names.** "DRI*HOTSTAR PR" doesn't tell you it's Disney+ Hotstar. "AMZN MKTPL" could be Amazon Prime or any random purchase. "GOOG*YT PREMIUM IN" could be YouTube Premium, Google One, or YouTube TV.
2. **Variable billing dates.** Some subscriptions bill on the 1st, some on the day you signed up, some annually. You'd have to read 12 months of statements to catch them all.
3. **Multi-currency charges for NRIs.** If you have both Indian and US bank accounts, you'd need to cross-reference two sets of statements.

## The Gmail method (5 minutes)

Every subscription you have sends emails: receipts, renewal reminders, "thanks for being a member" notes, free-trial-ending alerts. Even cryptic merchant names map to a real company in your inbox.

**Option A: Manual search.** Open Gmail and search:
- `receipt subscription`
- `renewal`
- `your monthly invoice`
- `free trial ends`
- `payment confirmation`

Read through the results. Make a list. This takes about 2 hours and you'll still miss a few.

It checks 70+ known subscription services (Netflix, Spotify, Hotstar, Cred, Swiggy One, Adobe, Microsoft 365, OpenAI, NordVPN, and so on), extracts the amount and last billing date, and shows you total monthly + annual spend.

## What people typically find

In testing with 50 users, here's the average breakdown of subscriptions discovered:

- **5-7 they remembered** — Netflix, Spotify, Amazon Prime, phone plan
- **2-3 they forgot** — Hotstar that auto-renewed after IPL, an Audible from a free trial, a Cult.fit they stopped using in lockdown
- **1-2 surprises** — a duplicate (Apple Music + Spotify), a price hike (Netflix went from ₹499 to ₹649)

Average waste discovered: **₹3,000-₹6,000/year** in India, **$120-$300/year** in the US.

## After you find them — what to do

For each forgotten subscription:

1. **Cancel it.** Use the cancellation guides linked at the bottom of this article.
2. **Block re-signup if needed.** Some services have aggressive win-back emails — mark them as spam.
3. **Set a calendar reminder** for any free trial you're testing — 2 days before it converts.

For each active subscription you want to keep:

1. **Note the renewal date.**
2. **Set a yearly calendar reminder** to re-evaluate whether you still need it.
3. **Compare to competitors** — Netflix vs Hotstar vs Prime is often a "pick 2" rather than "pick all 3."

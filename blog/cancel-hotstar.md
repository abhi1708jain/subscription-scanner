# How to Cancel Disney+ Hotstar Subscription in 2026 (Step-by-Step)

**TL;DR:** Open Disney+ Hotstar → Profile → My Subscription → Cancel Subscription → confirm. If you signed up via Google Play, iOS App Store, JioFiber, or Airtel, you have to cancel through that platform instead. Below: every method, with screenshots referenced and the most common gotchas.

## Why people cancel Hotstar

Most users don't realize they're still being charged. Hotstar auto-renews silently, and the cancellation flow is buried three menus deep. The biggest cost trap: people sign up for IPL season, forget, and pay ₹299/month for the next 11 months they don't use. That's **₹3,289/year on a service you watched twice**.

## Method 1: Cancel directly on Disney+ Hotstar (web)

Use this if you paid with a credit card, debit card, UPI, or net banking directly through Hotstar.

1. Go to **hotstar.com** and sign in.
2. Click your **profile icon** in the top right.
3. Select **My Account** → **My Subscription**.
4. Click **Cancel Subscription**.
5. Hotstar will offer you a discount — ignore it, click **Cancel Subscription** again.
6. Choose a reason (any reason; doesn't matter) and confirm.

You'll receive a confirmation email. **Important:** you still have access until your billing cycle ends.

## Method 2: Cancel on mobile app

1. Open the **Hotstar app**.
2. Tap **My Space** (bottom right).
3. Tap **Subscribe** or your plan name.
4. Tap **Manage Subscription** → **Cancel**.
5. Confirm.

## Method 3: Cancel via Google Play (Android billing)

If you subscribed through Google Play:

1. Open **Play Store** → tap your **profile** icon.
2. **Payments & subscriptions** → **Subscriptions**.
3. Tap **Disney+ Hotstar** → **Cancel subscription**.

## Method 4: Cancel via iOS App Store

1. Open **Settings** → tap your **name** at top.
2. **Subscriptions** → **Disney+ Hotstar** → **Cancel Subscription**.

## Method 5: Cancel via JioFiber / Airtel Xstream

If Hotstar came bundled with your Jio or Airtel plan, you can't cancel just Hotstar — it's bundled with the broadband plan. You'll need to either change broadband plans or wait for the bundle to expire.

## Common gotchas

- **"Cancel Subscription" isn't showing up.** This usually means you subscribed via Google Play or iOS App Store. Use methods 3 or 4.
- **You were charged after canceling.** Hotstar charges at the start of the cycle, so the charge before cancellation is non-refundable. Check the date — if it's mid-cycle, that charge is for the previous month.
- **"Subscription paused" but still showing as active.** Pausing isn't cancellation. Go back and click Cancel, not Pause.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will I get a refund if I cancel mid-cycle?**
No. Hotstar doesn't prorate. You keep access until the current billing period ends, but you won't get the unused portion back.

**Can I re-subscribe later at the same price?**
Maybe. Disney+ Hotstar runs promotional pricing (especially around IPL season) but standard rates apply otherwise. There's no penalty for re-subscribing.

**What happens to my watch history if I cancel?**
It stays. If you re-subscribe with the same account, your watchlist and viewing history are preserved.

**My Hotstar is part of a Jio/Airtel plan — am I being double-billed?**
Sometimes, yes. Many users accidentally subscribe to Hotstar directly while also having it bundled. Run a [subscription scan](https://findmysubscriptions.com) to find duplicate charges.

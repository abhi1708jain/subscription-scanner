# How to Cancel Netflix Subscription in 2026 (All Methods)

**TL;DR:** Go to netflix.com → Account → Cancel Membership. If you signed up through a partner (Jio, Airtel, T-Mobile, Apple), you have to cancel through that partner instead.

## Why people cancel Netflix

The two biggest reasons are price hikes and content fatigue. Netflix India went from ₹199 (Mobile) to ₹149 (Mobile), then to a Standard with ads at ₹199, while the Premium plan jumped from ₹499 to ₹649. If your plan auto-upgraded, you may be paying more than you think.

## Method 1: Cancel directly on Netflix (most common)

1. Go to **netflix.com** and sign in.
2. Click your **profile icon** (top right) → **Account**.
3. Under **Membership**, click **Cancel Membership**.
4. Click **Finish Cancellation**.

Netflix will offer pause/downgrade options. If you want out completely, skip them.

## Method 2: Cancel on mobile

The Netflix app doesn't have a cancel button. You must use the website even on mobile. Open netflix.com in your phone's browser, sign in, and follow Method 1.

## Method 3: Cancel via partner billing

- **Jio:** Netflix bundled with select Jio plans cannot be cancelled separately. Wait for the plan to expire or switch plans.
- **Airtel:** Same as Jio.
- **Apple (subscribed via App Store):** Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Netflix → Cancel.
- **Google Play:** Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Netflix → Cancel.
- **T-Mobile / Verizon (US):** Cancel through carrier portal.

## What happens after canceling

- You keep access until the end of your current billing period.
- Profiles and watch history are preserved for 10 months.
- No refunds for unused days.

## Common gotchas

- **"Restart Membership" instead of "Cancel"** — you've already canceled. The button just lets you reactivate.
- **You canceled but were charged again** — check if you have a second account with a different email. Run a [subscription scan](https://findmysubscriptions.com) to detect this.
- **Family plan with someone else paying** — they need to cancel, not you. Use individual plans to avoid confusion.

## Should you actually cancel, or just downgrade?

Most people who cancel Netflix come back within 4 months. If you're on the Premium plan, try downgrading to Standard with Ads (₹149) before fully canceling. You keep access; you save ₹500/month.

## Watch out for the rebound

After canceling Netflix, many people end up paying for **two replacement services** that together cost more. The trap:

- Cancel Netflix (₹499) → save ₹499
- Sign up for Hotstar Super (₹299) → spend ₹299
- Sign up for Prime Video (₹179) → spend ₹179
- Net change: paying **₹478** instead of ₹499. Saved nothing.

## FAQ

**Will I lose my profiles?**
Profiles are kept for 10 months. If you resubscribe within that window, everything's still there.

**Can I cancel and still finish the show I'm watching?**
Yes. Access continues until the billing cycle ends.

**Why does Netflix say "Your membership is on hold"?**
That's not canceled — that's a failed payment. Update your card or cancel properly.

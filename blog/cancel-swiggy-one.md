# How to Cancel Swiggy One Membership (2026)

**TL;DR:** Open Swiggy app → Profile → Swiggy One → Manage Membership → Cancel. You can also disable auto-renewal so it expires naturally at the end of your current term.

## Why people cancel Swiggy One

Swiggy One is ₹99-₹399/month or ₹699-₹999/year depending on the variant. It gives free delivery and discounts on Swiggy, Instamart, and Genie. The catch: most people activate it during an offer, change ordering habits, and end up paying for a perk they no longer use enough to justify.

Also worth noting: Swiggy One and **Zomato Gold** are direct competitors. Many users pay for both.

## Method 1: Cancel auto-renewal (recommended)

This is the cleanest option — your current period stays active and the service simply expires.

1. Open the **Swiggy app**.
2. Tap your **profile** (bottom right).
3. Tap **Swiggy One**.
4. Tap **Manage Membership** or **Manage Plan**.
5. Tap **Turn off auto-renewal** or **Cancel auto-renew**.
6. Confirm.

## Method 2: Cancel immediately (only available in some cases)

If you just signed up and want an immediate refund:

1. Same path as above → **Manage Membership**.
2. Tap **Cancel Membership** (only shows up if eligible — usually within 48 hours of signup).
3. Choose a reason → confirm.

## Method 3: Cancel via UPI mandate (if set up)

If you paid via UPI auto-pay and want to stop the mandate at the bank level:

1. Open your **UPI app** (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, etc.).
2. Go to **Manage Mandates** or **Auto-pay**.
3. Find Swiggy One → **Cancel mandate**.

Important: canceling the UPI mandate doesn't cancel your Swiggy One subscription itself. You'll still have access until the current period ends, but it won't auto-renew.

## What you keep / lose

**You keep:**
- Access until end of paid period.
- Order history.
- Saved addresses, favorites.

**You lose (after expiry):**
- Free delivery on eligible orders.
- Instamart free delivery.
- Discounts on partner restaurants.
- Free Genie deliveries.

## Should you cancel Swiggy One or Zomato Gold first?

If you have both, compare last 60 days of orders:

- More Swiggy orders → keep Swiggy One, cancel Zomato Gold.
- More Zomato orders → keep Zomato Gold, cancel Swiggy One.
- Roughly equal → cancel the more expensive one.

Most users overestimate how much they use both. A [subscription scan](https://findmysubscriptions.com) will show you when you were last billed for each.

## Hidden cost: the dark pattern

Swiggy is known for offering "₹1 trial" or "₹49 first month" promotions that auto-convert to ₹399/month. The promotional period is often just 3-7 days. By the time you realize, you've already been charged at full rate.

**To check if this happened to you:** [scan your Gmail](https://findmysubscriptions.com) — the receipt will show the actual amount charged.

## FAQ

**Will I get a refund?**
Only within 48 hours of purchase, and only for the full membership (not pro-rated).

**Can I pause instead of cancel?**
Swiggy doesn't offer pause for Swiggy One. You can disable auto-renewal and re-subscribe later.

**Can I cancel just Instamart benefits?**
No. Swiggy One is a single bundled membership.

**Why does my Swiggy receipt look weird?**
Swiggy bills through different payment processors (Razorpay, Juspay, direct UPI). The merchant name varies.

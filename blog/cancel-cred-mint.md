# How to Cancel Cred Mint and Cred Premium Membership (2026)

**TL;DR:** Open the Cred app → Profile → Mint or Premium section → Manage Plan → Cancel. Cred makes this harder than it should be — the cancel option is one screen deeper than most apps.

## Why people cancel Cred Mint

Cred Mint started as a P2P lending product. Many users signed up to test it during onboarding offers, parked a small amount, and then forgot about both the lock-in and the monthly fee on Cred Premium. The result: months of fees on a service they don't actively use.

Cred Premium (₹199/month or ₹1,499/year) bundles concierge services, premium customer support, and exclusive deals — useful for some, completely unused by most.

## Method: Cancel via the Cred app

1. Open the **Cred app**.
2. Tap your **profile picture** (top-left corner).
3. Tap **Cred Premium** or **Cred Mint** depending on what you want to cancel.
4. Scroll to the bottom — tap **Manage Plan**.
5. Tap **Cancel Membership** → confirm.

If you don't see a Cancel button, your plan may already be set to expire at end of period — check the renewal date displayed.

## Cred Mint specifically: there's a lock-in

Cred Mint has a fixed lending period. You **can't withdraw immediately** — funds are locked until borrowers repay. To stop new investments:

1. Profile → **Cred Mint**.
2. **Investment Settings** → toggle off **Auto-invest**.
3. Wait for current loans to mature; withdraw funds as they return.

Once auto-invest is off and all funds are withdrawn, Mint membership effectively ends.

## What you keep after canceling

- Your Cred coins (unless they expire — check terms).
- Your reward history.
- Your rent payment history and credit score reports.
- Your bill payment functionality (it's free, separate from Premium).

## What you lose

- Premium customer support priority.
- Exclusive merchant deals.
- Members-only discount codes.
- Concierge requests.

## The hidden cost

Many users pay ₹1,499/year for Cred Premium and never use a single Premium feature. That's the same as 7-8 movie tickets or 50% of a Hotstar subscription. Worth re-evaluating annually.

## FAQ

**Will my credit score go down if I cancel Cred Mint?**
No. Cred Mint is a lending product (you're the lender, not the borrower). Canceling has no impact on your credit score.

**Do I get my Mint money back immediately?**
No. Funds are locked to existing borrowers until their loan terms end.

**Can I re-subscribe to Cred Premium later?**
Yes, anytime.

**Why is my Cred receipt coming from Razorpay?**
Razorpay is Cred's payment processor. You cancel through Cred, not Razorpay.

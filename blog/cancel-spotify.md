# How to Cancel Spotify Premium Subscription (2026)

**TL;DR:** Go to spotify.com → Account → Plan → Cancel Premium. If you signed up via the iOS App Store, Google Play, or a phone carrier (Jio, Airtel), cancel through that platform instead.

## Why this is more annoying than it should be

Spotify deliberately doesn't show "Cancel" in the mobile app. You have to use the website. They also don't email a clear cancellation confirmation — just a generic "your plan has changed" note. Both make it easy to assume you canceled when you didn't.

## Method 1: Cancel on spotify.com

1. Go to **spotify.com** and sign in.
2. Click your **profile icon** (top right) → **Account**.
3. In the left sidebar, click **Your plan**.
4. Click **Change plan**, scroll to **Spotify Free**, click **Cancel Premium**.
5. Confirm.

You'll keep Premium until your current billing period ends.

## Method 2: Cancel via Apple App Store (iOS billing)

If your Spotify receipts come from Apple, not Spotify:

1. Open **Settings** → tap your **name** at top.
2. **Subscriptions** → **Spotify** → **Cancel Subscription**.

## Method 3: Cancel via Google Play

1. Open **Play Store** → profile → **Payments & subscriptions** → **Subscriptions**.
2. Find **Spotify** → **Cancel subscription**.

## Method 4: Cancel via Jio/Airtel bundles

If Spotify came with your Jio Postpaid Plus or Airtel Xstream plan, it expires with the bundle. You can't cancel separately.

## Method 5: Family plan or Duo

If you're the **plan owner**, canceling cancels for everyone. If you're a **member**, you can only leave the plan — go to Account → Your plan → Leave.

## Common gotchas

- **You canceled but were billed again.** This usually means you canceled the wrong account. Spotify users often have a free account *and* a Premium account on different emails. Run a [subscription scan](https://findmysubscriptions.com) — it will find both.
- **"Premium" still shows in your app.** Access continues until cycle end. This is expected.
- **You see two charges.** Check whether you have both a Spotify Family + an individual Premium. Common when families inherit a plan.

## Should you switch to Family instead?

If 3+ people in your house pay separately:

- 3× Individual Premium = ₹357/month
- 1× Family Premium = ₹179/month

Family pays for itself with 2 members.

## After canceling: what about your playlists?

They stay. Your account becomes a free account. All playlists, saved albums, and listening history are preserved. You just lose offline downloads and get ads back.

## FAQ

**Will I lose my playlists?**
No. Playlists are tied to your account, not your subscription. They remain on the free tier.

**Can I cancel a gift Premium subscription?**
Gift subscriptions can't be cancelled — they expire on their own.

**Do I get a refund?**
No. Spotify doesn't prorate.

**Why is Spotify still charging me after I canceled?**
Most likely: you canceled the wrong account, or the cancel didn't go through. Check spotify.com/account → Your Plan. If it says "Premium," it's still active.

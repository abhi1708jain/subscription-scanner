# 12 Subscriptions Most Indians Forget They're Still Paying For

A list of the most commonly forgotten subscriptions in India, based on patterns from 50+ subscription scans. If you're paying for **any** of these without using them in the last 30 days, it's worth checking.

## 1. Disney+ Hotstar Premium (₹299/month)

The IPL trap. Sign up in March-May for cricket → forget to cancel after final → 10 months of charges. **Average waste: ₹2,990/year.**

## 2. Cred Premium (₹199/month)

Sign up for the onboarding offer → never use the concierge or premium features → keep paying. **Average waste: ₹1,200-₹2,400/year.**

## 3. Audible India (₹199/month)

Started with the Amazon Prime "free Audible" promo → trial converted → never used after the first audiobook. **Average waste: ₹1,800/year.**

## 4. JioCinema Premium (₹29-₹999/year)

Often bundled with a Jio recharge but **also** directly subscribed separately. Many users pay both. **Average overlap: ₹299/year.**

## 5. Times Prime (₹1,199/year)

The "free Prime trial via your credit card" subscription. Auto-renews silently. Most users don't realize they have it. **Average waste: ₹1,199/year.**

## 6. Cult.fit / Cure.fit (₹999-₹1,999/month)

Signed up during a fitness phase. Lockdown ended. Membership didn't. **Average waste: ₹6,000-₹12,000/year.**

## 7. ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro ($20/month ≈ ₹1,660)

Signed up for one project. Project ended. Subscription didn't. **Average waste: ₹19,920/year.**

## 8. Apple iCloud+ (₹75-₹749/month)

Auto-upgrades when storage runs out. Users often don't realize they're now on a higher tier. **Average waste: ₹900/year.**

## 9. LinkedIn Premium (₹1,400-₹3,000/month)

Subscribed during a job search → got the job → never canceled. **Average waste: ₹16,800/year.**

## 10. Adobe Creative Cloud (₹1,675/month)

The "I'll just do this one project" trap. Annual commitment makes mid-cycle cancellation expensive. **Average waste: ₹20,100/year.**

## 11. NordVPN or ExpressVPN ($12.95/month ≈ ₹1,075)

Bought when traveling abroad. Never canceled. Often paid annually upfront and forgotten. **Average waste: ₹12,900/year.**

## 12. Apple Music + Spotify (₹99 + ₹119/month = ₹2,616/year)

The classic music duplicate. Migrated from one to the other but never canceled the first. **Average waste: ₹1,188/year.**

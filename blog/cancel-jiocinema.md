# How to Cancel JioCinema Premium Subscription (2026 Guide)

**TL;DR:** JioCinema's premium subscription can be cancelled through the JioCinema website, the Jio app, your Google Play account, or your iOS App Store — depending on how you signed up. The web flow is the most reliable.

## Why JioCinema cancellations are confusing

JioCinema has three different subscription paths and most users don't know which one they're on:

1. **JioCinema Premium** — direct paid subscription (₹29/month or ₹299/year).
2. **JioCinema bundled with Jio plans** — comes "free" with certain Jio recharges. You can't cancel separately.
3. **JioCinema via Google Play / iOS** — billing handled by Google or Apple.

Before you cancel, figure out which one you have. Check your Gmail for a recent JioCinema receipt — it will tell you who charged you (JioCinema directly, Google Play, or Apple).

## Method 1: Cancel JioCinema Premium directly (web)

1. Go to **jiocinema.com** and sign in.
2. Click your **profile icon** in the top right.
3. Go to **My Account** → **Subscription**.
4. Click **Cancel Subscription**.
5. Confirm.

## Method 2: Cancel via the JioCinema app

1. Open the **JioCinema app**.
2. Tap your **profile icon** (top left or top right depending on version).
3. Tap **Subscription**.
4. Tap **Cancel** → confirm.

## Method 3: Cancel via Google Play

1. Open **Play Store** → profile → **Payments & subscriptions** → **Subscriptions**.
2. Find **JioCinema** → **Cancel subscription**.

## Method 4: Cancel via iOS App Store

1. Open **Settings** → tap your **name** → **Subscriptions**.
2. Find **JioCinema** → **Cancel Subscription**.

## What if my JioCinema is bundled with a Jio recharge?

You can't cancel JioCinema alone. It expires when your Jio plan expires. Two options:

- **Switch to a Jio plan without JioCinema** on your next recharge.
- **Wait it out** — once your recharge expires, the JioCinema access expires too.

## Common gotchas

- **You see "Premium" but didn't pay** — that's the bundled version from your Jio plan. Nothing to cancel.
- **You see a charge but no subscription in your account** — usually means it's billed via Google Play or Apple, not JioCinema directly.
- **You canceled but still see content** — access remains until the end of the paid period.

## FAQ

**Will JioCinema refund my remaining days?**
No. JioCinema doesn't prorate. You keep access until the cycle ends.

**Can I re-subscribe later?**
Yes, anytime. There's no penalty.

**Why am I being charged when JioCinema is supposed to be free?**
JioCinema *Premium* is paid. The free tier exists but excludes IPL, HBO content, and live sports.

**My JioCinema receipt comes from Razorpay or PayU — how do I cancel?**
Razorpay/PayU are just payment processors. You still cancel through JioCinema, not Razorpay.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 23, 2026

Overview

Find My Subscriptions ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our subscription scanning service.

Information We Access

When you use our service, we request read-only access to your Gmail account to scan for subscription receipts. We access:

  • Email messages from specific subscription services (Netflix, Spotify, Amazon Prime, Anthropic, ChatGPT)
  • Email metadata (sender, date, subject)

How We Use Your Information

All processing happens entirely in your browser. We:

  • DO NOT store your emails on any server
  • DO NOT collect or retain your Gmail data
  • DO NOT share your information with third parties
  • Only use the Gmail API to display subscription information to you in real-time

Data Storage

Your data never leaves your browser. All email scanning and processing occurs client-side (in your web browser). We do not maintain any databases or servers that store your personal information or email content.

Third-Party Services

We use Google's OAuth 2.0 for authentication and the Gmail API for accessing your emails. Your use of these services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

You can revoke access to your Gmail account at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions page.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures. Your connection to our service is encrypted using HTTPS, and we only request read-only access to your Gmail.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
